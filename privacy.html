Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Hypso25 handles basic information when you visit our website.

Information we collect

This website is mainly informational. We do not collect account registrations, online orders or payment information through this website.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not save anything in your browser.

Google Maps

The Contact page can show a map from Google Maps. The map does not load until you click "Show map". After you click, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and may set cookies, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The "Open in Google Maps" link takes you to Google's website.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). To deliver the pages and keep the service secure, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Analytics and tracking

This website does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar tracking tools. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

Links to other websites

Links to other websites, such as Facebook, Instagram or Google Maps, take you to those websites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Contact

If you contact us by phone, email, social media or another external platform, your information is handled through that communication channel.
