Our Story

In the heart of Constanta, from the depths of the sea, where mysteries abound and creatures of wonder lurk, there lies a hidden gem known as Hypso25. Here, the enchanting fusion of a marine bistro and a steampunk wonderland unfolds. With a passion for the extraordinary, we bring you a unique experience unlike any other. Dive into the depths with our specialty coffees and tantalize your taste buds with our creatively crafted cocktails. Let the enigmas of the deep waters transport you to a realm where the impossible becomes possible. Welcome to Hypso25, where every sip tells a tale and every moment is an adventure.
