Our Story

At Hypso25, our marine bistro with a steampunk twist is a whimsical oasis located at 11 Piata Ovidiu in the heart of Constanta. As you step into our enchanting space, you'll be greeted by the alluring aroma of specialty coffee brewing and the tantalizing sight of expertly crafted cocktails being prepared behind our vintage bar.

Our story began with a vision to blend the mysteries of the deep waters with the creativity of steampunk design, creating a unique sanctuary for coffee enthusiasts and cocktail connoisseurs alike. The fusion of these two distinct worlds is evident in every detail of our decor, from the intricate clockwork gears adorning the walls to the nautical artifacts scattered throughout the bistro.

Each sip of our specialty coffee tells a tale of craftsmanship and dedication, sourced from the finest beans and brewed to perfection by our skilled baristas. For those seeking a more adventurous experience, our creative cocktail menu offers imaginative elixirs that push the boundaries of flavor, taking your taste buds on a thrilling journey through the depths of mixology.

Whether you're stopping by for your morning cup of joe or unwinding with friends over handcrafted cocktails in the evening, Hypso25 invites you to embrace the enchantment of the sea and the marvels of steampunk while enjoying a truly one-of-a-kind experience in the heart of Constanta.
